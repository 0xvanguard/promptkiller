ℹ️ About PromptKiller

🛡️ What is PromptKiller?

PromptKiller is a comprehensive AI red teaming toolkit containing 501 adversarial prompts across 15 attack categories. It's designed for security researchers, AI safety teams, and red teamers to test and harden AI systems against prompt injection, jailbreaking, and manipulation attempts.
